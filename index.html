Frequently Asked

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know about shopping with Besty Food. Can't find your answer? Reach out to our support team — we're happy to help.

Customer Support
support@bestyfood.com
Besty Food is an online grocery and food delivery platform that connects customers with participating local stores and food merchants. Browse everyday groceries, fresh produce, and food, and have them delivered to your doorstep where service is available.
Browse the categories or search for the products you need, add them to your cart, choose your delivery and payment options, and place your order. You'll receive updates on your order status through the platform.
Delivery availability depends on your area and the participating stores near you. Use the delivery checker on this page or check the Besty Food platform at checkout to confirm whether delivery is supported for your PIN code.
Besty Food supports the common digital and online payment methods available in your area. Available options will be shown at checkout based on your location and the participating store.
You can reach Besty Food customer support through the contact options on the platform or at support@bestyfood.com. The support team can help with orders, account questions, and any issues you may have while shopping.
